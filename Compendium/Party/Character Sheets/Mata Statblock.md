### FEATURES

#### Combat

***Not This Time - Action:*** **Spend 3 Hope** to force an adversary within Far range to re-roll and attack or damage roll.

<input type="checkbox"> ***Rune War - Reaction :*** When hit by an attack, spend a Hope to reduce the damage by **1d8**. On an 8, this cannot be used again until you rest.

<input type="checkbox"> ***Counterspell - Reaction :*** You can interrupt a magical effect taking place by making a Knowledge reaction roll. On a success, the effect stops and any consequences are avoided, and this cannot be used again until you rest.

#### General

***Internal Compass - Passive:*** When you roll a 1 on your Hope Die, you can re-roll it.

***Strange Patterns - Passive:*** When you roll a 12 on a Duality Dice, gain a Hope or clear a Stress.

***Adept - Reaction:*** When you utilise an Experience, you can **mark a Stress** instead of spending a Hope. If you do, double the Experience modifier for that roll.

#### Utility

***Prestidigitation - Action:*** You can perform harmless, subtle magical effects at will. For example, you can change an object’s colour, create a smell, light a candle, cause a tiny object to float, illuminate a room, or repair a small object

<input type="checkbox"> ***Wall Walk - Action :*** **Spend a Hope** to allow a creature you can touch to climb walls and ceilings until the end of the scene or you cast this again.

***Floating Eye - Action :*** **Spend a Hope** to create a single, small floating orb that you can move anywhere within Very Far range. While this spell is active, you can see through the orb as though you’re looking out from its position. You can transition between using your own senses and seeing through the orb freely. If the orb takes damage or moves out of range, the spell ends.
