### Description
#### Background
Once the most beautiful singer in [[Lothlonde]], she became infamous across the world for her enchanting songs. Until people started to whisper she was a better singer than [[Melodious]] themselves, angering the god greatly. They challenged her to a contest, and while [[Melodious]] was judged to be technically more proficient, Alluvia embed her songs with more emotion. This entranced the mortal judges more, who declared her the winner. In a fit of rage, [[Melodious]] cursed Alluvia to become the first [[Siren]], filling her with a hunger that could only be quenched by luring mortals to her with her singing. 

#### Motivations
- Feed her hunger
- End her curse
- Take revenge on the gods
