### Description
The Amulet of Dominate Beast is used by [[Ithlen]] to enslave [[Laune]]. It allows her to exert her will on [[Laune]] and use her Aura of Life to enrich [[Camarath]].

In [[Arc 2 Session 3]], [[Mayastan Daar]] saw the chain of the amulet around her neck while looking for another [[Amulet of Nystul]].
