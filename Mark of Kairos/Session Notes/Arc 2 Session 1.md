### Summary
[[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] was woken up as normal, and failed to convince the maid or [[Jay]] to leave the city. While walking to meet [[Phoenix Aevum|Phoenix]], she and [[Mata Starheart|Mata]] discussed what needed to be done now they were in the final loop. Mata had decided that the main priority was to stop or [[Delay The Ritual]], having broken it down into three aims: preventing the resurrection of [[Comrath]], prevent the map of [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/The Dark Storm/The Dark Storm]] being created by evacuating all the astral scryers, and stopping them making the [[Bottle of Age]] - potentially by destroying the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Tellus/Lothlonde/Hestrela/Camarath/Hill District/Shrine to Kairos/Shrine to Kairos]]. When they met [[Phoenix Aevum|Phoenix]] they tried to work out how to convince people to evacuate [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Tellus/Lothlonde/Hestrela/Camarath/Camarath]], the obvious people to go to are the city leaders; [[Ithlen]] - City Head, [[Maera]] - Leader of the Guard or [[Cutha]] - Head of the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Tellus/Lothlonde/Hestrela/Camarath/Guild District/Adventurer's Guild/Adventurer's Guild]]. Eventually deciding on [[Cutha]], as the most likely to listen to them, to try to convince her to help them interrupt [[Anec]]'s cult meeting. 

At the [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Tellus/Lothlonde/Hestrela/Camarath/Guild District/Adventurer's Guild/Adventurer's Guild]] they decided to try to convince [[Ollir]] to let them see [[Cutha]] with minimal information and maximum earnestness, which somehow worked. Talking to [[Cutha]] went less well, telling her about the loop and [[Anec]]'s plans she initially dismissed them as making it all up. Until [[Mata Starheart|Mata]] remembered they still had [[Boda]]'s body which would show signs of being raised as undead. [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] decided to disappear into the [[Ring of Glikx]] and reappear with her body with no warning. After the initial shock of having a dead body dumped on her office floor, [[Cutha]] examined it and noticed the signs of undeath. Eventually deciding that this was sufficient enough to warrant investigation, and wanting a reason to get out of her duties, she agreed to accompany them to [[Cult of the Primeval]] meeting, but neglected to bring any backup. On arriving at [[Compendium/Atlas/Material Plane/Tellus/Lothlonde/Hestrela/Camarath/Hill District/Ulrach's Clock Shop/Ulrach's Clock Shop]] and finding it closed, they realised they never did work out a clean way to break in. But on further examination, [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] noticed a chimney, and calling on the power of [[Taranis]], she flew up and into the shop, able to open it from inside. Using the [[Primeval Token]] she put in the ring, [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] opened the secret entrance and, leaving [[Mata Starheart|Mata]] and [[Phoenix Aevum|Phoenix]] at the top so they could sneak better, she and [[Cutha]] snuck down to the shrine where they saw [[Anec]] tying up the unconscious bodies of the cult.
