###### End The Storm
<span class="sub2">:FasCircleExclamation: Quest | :FasListCheck: Ongoing</span>
___

### Description
The [[Kiwidy#Silent City of Kiwidy]] was once the sacred city of the storm god [[Tarhunz]] and a prosperous trading hub. But after [[Tarhunz]] and [[Aran]] created a storm of death that desolated [[Desolation of Suuhulla|Suuhulla]], [[Il'Zuk]] took revenge on the storm god. He hunted their followers until the god was weak enough that they took shelter in their temple in [[Kiwidy]]. But [[Il'Zuk]] stormed the temple and killed the god. Now, the last breath of [[Tarhunz]] haunts the temple and creates a perpetual howling storm in the city that is too loud to communicate verbally.

### Plan

#### Entrance
A storm rages outside that [[Taranis]] cannot end or shield [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] from as it is born of [[Tarhunz]]'s power. The winds/rain/lightning are at their fastest and most dangerous around the enterance.

#### Puzzle
The ground floor was the common area of [[Tarhunz]] temple. Storm symbology but the god appears different to [[Taranis]] with different religious iconography. Notably, [[Tarhunz]] is depicted with only 1 eye.

Skeletons of the ancient priests who defended [[Tarhunz]] remain. Some were raised as undead to fight each other so appear to be fighting amongst themselves.

The lifts are paternoster lifts designed to rotate by channelling air in the storm that runs through the hollow walls of the tower through a turbine. But the storm now rages only on the outside. If they can power the turbine or weigh down one of the lifts on the opposite side they would temporarily work again.

#### Trick or Setback
The lost souls of [[Desolation of Suuhulla|Suuhulla]] still remain here, used to power [[Il'Zuk]]. They can be spoken to and convinced to let the players reach the top floor and end the storm. They are initially distrustful due to his stormy nature, but will be more trusting learning he doesn't follow [[Tarhunz]]. They can tell the story of [[Tarhunz]] and [[Aran]]'s destruction of their homeland and how they helped to kill [[Tarhunz]] but cannot leave until their mission of eradicating [[Tarhunz]] is complete with the death of the [[Last Breath of Tarhunz]]. 

#### Combat
Fight against the [[Last Breath of Tarhunz]], start by summoning 1 [[Weakened Air Elemental]], roll to summon more later.
The top floor has a massive lightning rods that used to be used to power the temple but is now broken. The two halves of the wire are in the ceiling and floor at different places and will spark one per turn dealing 2d10 lightning damage to anyone on its path on an initiative rolled at the top of each turn.
There are holes in the floor.

#### Reward, Revelation or Twist
On killing the [[Last Breath of Tarhunz]] an artifact is revealed.
##### The Eye of the Storm

When using this as a spellcasting focus stormy magic becomes easier. When casting a spell of 5th level or lower to deal lightning or thunder damage or to affect the weather, roll a d4. On a 4 this spell does not consume a spell/pact slot.

You gain the ability to subtly control the weather around you.

If it is raining, you can use an action to cause the rain to stop falling in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on you. You can end this effect as a bonus action.

If it is windy, you can use a bonus action each round to choose the direction that the wind blows in a 100-foot-radius sphere centered on you. The wind blows in that direction until the end of your next turn. This feature doesn't alter the speed of the wind.
