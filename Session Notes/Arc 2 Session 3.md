### Summary
[[Anec]] was taken back to [[Ulrach's Clock Shop]], searched and thoroughly tied up and gagged. On his person was found the vial of drugs used on the [[Cult of the Primeval|Cult]], an [[Amulet of Nystul]] and the [[Entropic Dagger]]. When the [[Amulet of Nystul|Amulet]] was removed, [[Anec]] was revealed as an undead, with rotted flesh and exposed bone. The [[Entropic Dagger]] had the [[Mark of the Primevals|Mark of Scathach]] engraved on the blade and is imbued with entropic power to which [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] is also linked, it seems like she could attune to it. Deciding that destroying the [[Shrine to Kairos]] would likely have consequences, they would only do so as a last resort.

It was decided that [[Ithlen]] and [[Maera]] needed to be recruited, so [[Anec]] was put in a covered cart, found round the back of the shop, and taken to the [[Scholar's District]] where [[Cutha]] said [[Ithlen]] was located. She passed a message on to send some adventurer's to secure [[Ulrach's Clock Shop]] and check on the unconscious cult members tied up below.  [[Ithlen]] was busy conversing with VIPs from the [[Hestrela]] mainland, but was eventually convinced to come have a look at [[Anec]]. This convinced her to gather [[Maera]] and [[Tiva]] (to cast *Zone of Truth*) and meet them at the [[Hand of Twilight]] headquarters. [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] and [[Phoenix Aevum|Phoenix]] told their story under the truth zone, only leaving out some details about meeting [[Laune]] and the [[Hawker's Caverns]] as well as their suspicions about the other members of the [[Hand of Twilight]].

[[Anec]] resisted the spell however, so it was decided to send [[Ithlen]], [[Cutha]] and [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] to [[Anec's House]] to find his stash of potions, and specifically truth serum. They found his house full of decayed food and mould, with a secret passage in the bathroom leading down to an underground lair. [[Cutha]] climbed down first and fell victim to poisoned needles on the ladder, falling off but ultimately taking less damage than she could've. In the lair, [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] was able to find a cabinet full of various poisons, sedatives and what [[Ithlen]] identified as truth serum. [[Ithlen]] announced she'd found what appeared to be his diary at his writing desk and pocketed it to read later. Heading back to headquarters, [[Maera]] was asking [[Phoenix Aevum|Phoenix]] about fighting [[Il'Zuk]]. It was questioned whether the truth serum, but [[Ithlen]] said she wouldn't have gone along with it if she didn't think it would work, so it was administered. On initial questioning, [[Anec]] confirmed he was undead but claimed [[Mayastan Daar|Daar]] was the threat to [[Camarath]].
