### Summary
Daar was woken up as normal, and failed to convince the maid or Jay to leave the city. While walking to meet Phoenix, she and Mata discussed what needed to be done now they were in the final loop. Mata had decided that the main priority was to stop or Delay The Ritual, having broken it down into three aims: preventing the resurrection of Comrath, prevent the map of the dark storm being created by evacuating all the astral scryers, and stopping them making the bottle of age - potentially by destroying the shrine to Kairos. When the met Phoenix they tried to work out how to convince people to evacuate Camarath, the obvious people to go to are the city leaders; Ithlen - City Head, Maera - Leader of the Guard or Cutha - Head of the Adventurer's Guild. Eventually deciding on Cutha, as the most likely to listen to them and wanting to convince her to help them interrupt Anec's cult meeting. 

At the Adevnturer's Guild they decided to try to convince Ollir to let them see Cutha with minimal information and maximum earnestness, which somehow worked. Talking to Cutha went less well, telling her about the loop and Anec's plans she initially dismissed them as making it all up. Until Mata remembered they still had Boda's body which would show signs of being raised as undead. Daar decided to disappear into the ring and reappear with it with no warning. After the initial shock of having a dead body dumped on her office floor, Cutha examined it and noticed the signs of undeath. Eventually deciding that this was sufficient enough to warrant investigation, and wanting a reason to get out of her duties, she agreed to accompany them to Cult Meeting, but neglected to bring any backup. On arriving at Ulrach's Clock Shop and finding it closed, they realised they never did work out a clean way to break in. But on further examination, Daar noticed a chimney, and calling on the power of Taranis, she flew up and into the shop, able to open it from inside. Using the token she put in the ring, Daar opened the secret entrance and, leaving Mata and Phoenix at the top so they could sneak better, she and Cutha snuck down to the shrine where they saw Anec tying up the unconscious bodies of the cult.
