###### Sponsors

___

### Church of Trela
**Cluedo**
Gains and looses points based on identification of religious themes & symbology.

Will offer magical artifacts and godly blessings to the party, e.g.:
- [[Keepers Staff|Achiel's Staff]] ([[Stiltuc]])
- [[Hammer of Exota|Binur's Hammer]] ([[Sian]] or [[Bing Shmigton]])
- [[Afel's Feather]] ([[Boshominind]])
- [[Airblade Charm|Tarhunz Storm Stone]] ([[Sian]] or [[Bing Shmigton]])


[[Trela]] is governed by the church, [[Bing Shmigton]]'s forest was cut down to make ships for their holy war.

### Ostitech
**Cluedo**
Gains and looses points based on skill in combat and with weapons.

Will offer advanced (tier 2) weapons to the party, e.g.:
- [[Elder Bow]] ([[Bing Shmigton]])
- [[Improved Quarterstaff]] ([[Sian]])
- [[Improved Greatsword]] ([[Boshominind]])
- [[Improved Wand]] ([[Stiltuc]])

Provided the technology used by the Church for deforestation.
They stoke wars all across the continent to sell weapons, [[Sian]] will be familiar with their weaponry as used in the wars she seeks to end.

### Neutral Aid Program
**Cluedo**
Gains and looses points based on treatment of [[Guests]], opposition to gods and aligned morality.

Secretly run by [[Balaeth Grimforge]] with the aim of ending the games. Cannot offer anything to the players but will provide inside information about the games if chosen.
